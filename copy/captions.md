## Insider Secret Reel — “Ask them one question”

```
Here's a question worth asking before you put a deposit on anything in Cabo.

“Can I swim in the ocean in front of your resort?”

Along most of the twenty-mile Tourist Corridor the honest answer is no. Strong undertow, rock underfoot, tides that don't behave. It's not a secret and it's not a scandal — Grand Velas publishes it in their own FAQ, which is more than most will do. But nobody volunteers it while you're booking, and plenty of people find out at check-in with their swimsuit already on.

So the pools aren't a consolation prize in Cabo. They're the product. That changes which property is right for you, and it changes which room in it.

Don't take my word for any of this. Go and read the FAQ of whatever resort you're considering. If it doesn't answer the question, that's your answer.

📍 Los Cabos, Mexico

```
Three resorts in Los Cabos share a single driveway, and almost nobody books the right one.

They're all Velas. Grand Velas Los Cabos is the famous one — 304 suites, family, the AAA Five Diamond everybody's seen. Next door, Grand Velas Boutique opened in late 2023: 79 suites, adults only, and currently sitting on a perfect five out of five across 398 reviews. Behind both of them is Mar del Cabo — 46 rooms, sixteen-and-over, and genuinely boho in a way the other two aren't trying to be.

Two hundred and fifty metres separate all three.

Here's the part that matters: stay at the Boutique and you get ten restaurants — three of its own plus seven at the big sister next door, including the only Michelin-starred restaurant in Los Cabos, which is included in your rate.

So the question was never “Grand Velas, yes or no.” It was which of the three, and that depends entirely on who you're travelling with and what you want your mornings to sound like.

No filter on any booking site has ever asked you that.
