## Reel — “Ask them one question”
`REEL · 1080×1920` · **Education** · Instagram + Facebook · Week 4 Wed
> 👥 Contains FAM-group faces — cleared, but review before posting.

```
Something I'd want a friend to know before they book Cabo.

Ask the resort whether you can actually swim in the ocean out front. Along most of this coast the honest answer is no — undertow, rock, tides that move. It's not a scandal, and the good ones will tell you straight. But nobody volunteers it while you're booking, and I've watched people find out at check-in with their swimsuit already on.

So the pools here aren't a consolation prize. They're the whole point, and that changes which resort is right for you and which room in it.

Don't take my word for it. Go read the FAQ of wherever you're looking. If it won't answer the question, that's your answer.

📍 Los Cabos, Mexico

#loscabos #cabosanlucas #traveladvisor
```

## Feed — which of the three
`FEED POST · 1080×1350` · **Education** · Instagram + Facebook · Week 4 Fri

```
Here's the thing about Grand Velas in Los Cabos that nobody explains.

It isn't one resort. It's three, side by side, sharing a driveway.

The big one is the famous one — families, kids in the pools, brilliant if that's your week. Next door is the grown-ups one, much smaller, very quiet. Behind both of them is a little boho place that feels nothing like either.

Same beach. Same kitchens. Completely different holidays.

So when someone says “we're looking at Grand Velas,” my first question isn't whether — it's which one, and who's coming with you. That's a two-minute conversation that decides your entire week, and no booking site has ever asked it.

📍 Los Cabos, Mexico

#loscabos #cabosanlucas #traveladvisor
```
